# GRK Kennel — Canine Facility, Boarding & Provisions (Vijayawada)

Built with **React**, **Vite**, **Tailwind CSS v4**, and **Lucide Icons** following the **Unslop design direction** (CC BY 4.0 by Damir Sokolovsky).

---

## 1. Brand & Design Foundation

- **Primary Brand Accent:** Deep Crimson Burgundy (`#790B13`)
- **Foundational Surface:** Warm Ivory Cream (`#FFF0DC`)
- **Supporting Neutral Ramp:**
  - Page Backgrounds: `#FFF0DC` & `#FAF5EB`
  - Elevated Cards: `#FFFBF4`
  - Primary Text: Deep Espresso Ink (`#1F1B16`)
  - Secondary Text: Warm Loam (`#5E554A`)
  - Hairlines & Borders: `rgba(31, 27, 22, 0.08)`
  - Contrast Ratio: > 9:1 (WCAG AAA compliant)
- **Typography:**
  - Display / Headlines: *Fraunces* (Editorial Serif, 400–500 weight)
  - UI / Body: *Plus Jakarta Sans* (Human Sans, 400–500 weight)
  - Numerals & Meta: *JetBrains Mono* / Tabular figures
- **Signature Detail:**
  - **The Debossed Crimson Kennel Seal (`#790B13`)**: SVG heraldic mark (`public/seal.svg`) with delicate hairline brass ring (`#C69A45`), stamped on verified lineage records, boarding check-ins, and nutrition batches.

---

## 2. All 5 Business Offerings Covered

1. **Puppies for Sale (Ethical Adoption):**
   - Purebred show-line & working lines (Golden Retriever, Labrador, German Shepherd, Beagle, Shih Tzu).
   - Mother-weaned 60+ days, microchipped, complete vaccination schedules, and KCI registration documentation.
2. **Pet Boarding & Daycare:**
   - 3 Lodging Tiers: Standard Air-Cooled Suite, Deluxe Garden Paddock Suite, Senior & Medical Care Suite.
   - Paddock runs, climate-controlled comfort for Vijayawada weather, daily WhatsApp video logs for pet parents.
3. **Pet Food & Nutrition:**
   - Veterinary-grade grain-free formulas for puppies, active adults, sensitive digestions, and senior joint mobility.
4. **Pet Bedding:**
   - Elevated cooling mesh cots (prevents floor heat & skin hotspots in coastal AP), orthopedic memory foam canvas beds, waterproof crate mats.
5. **Pet Accessories:**
   - Full-grain bridle leather leads with solid brass snap clips, ergonomic no-pull chest harnesses, weighted non-tip stainless bowls, natural rubber training toys.

## 3. Key Interactive Moments

- **The Boarding & Daily Routine Ledger:**
  - Time-stamped schedule card tracking a boarding guest's full 24-hour cycle (06:30 AM to 08:30 PM).
  - Clear protocol breakdown: Vaccination checks, tick/flea quarantine, continuous fresh RO hydration, CCTV monitoring.
- **The Five-Pillar Provisions & Lineage Index:**
  - Filterable interactive tab view for all 5 business pillars with pricing in INR (₹) and direct inquiry actions.
- **Direct WhatsApp & Phone Inquiry Modal:**
  - Pre-populates structured WhatsApp messages with service type, pet breed/age, dates, and owner notes for instant submission to `+91 94943 28999`.
  - One-click "Copy Inquiry Summary" clipboard feature.
- **Vijayawada Facility Dispatch:**
  - Directions for Kanuru–Enikepadu Link Road, visiting guidelines, operating hours (8:30 AM – 7:30 PM daily), and pet taxi pickup availability across Vijayawada, Guntur, and Amaravati.
